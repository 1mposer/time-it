## Owner rulings this lane implements (all 2026-11-15/16)
- **Ring caption "Average"** — the day score's user-facing name (replaces "Score"). **"Tomorrow"** on a ring that shows tomorrow because today's hours have passed.
- **Ring colour by score band**: ≥70 `rating/perfect` green, 40–69 `rating/good` orange, <40 `rating/bad` red. (The shipped tint-by-verdict is replaced; the verdict keeps its own colour on the verdict line.) The score never feeds a verdict (ADR-0011 independence).
- **Card** (owner's picks + edit rounds): ring column (ring + caption) · name row (icon in a fixed slot, name, chevron) · hour strip (tier-coloured segments) · hour integers under the bars · the **verdict line** "7 – 10am · Perfect" in the verdict's tint, **under the hour numbers, with padding** (null day: "No good time" in red) · week dots running the **full card width**. **No Range pill, no "Best hours" words, no own-hours line on the card** (04 a).
- **After today's hours pass** (05 a): the card shows **tomorrow** (`days[1]`), ring caption "Tomorrow", today's week dot greyed, under a **glass overlay** that covers that card only: "Your chosen hours of 6 – 10am have passed, you are seeing tomorrow's score." + "Tap to dismiss". Tap dismisses it for the rest of that local day (per Activity). The detail opens on tomorrow with the **"Today's hours are over" row** on top: check glyph + "Best was 7 – 10am · Perfect" (06 a; fallback when the morning result is unknown: the title alone). Tap → light haptic → green outline + green-filled check (0.2 s) → the row **slides off to the right** and the content rises (0.3 s spring); Reduce Motion: cross-fade. Dismissed for the rest of that day (per Activity). Today stays reachable from its greyed dot (no row there).
- **Retained morning result**: the client keeps, per Activity per local date, the last non-null day-0 result seen before the hours passed (verdict + window label), so the row can say "Best was …". Local, never sent.
- **Detail hero** with a **Report / Explore switch** (glass segmented control, `chart.bar.doc.horizontal` / `slider.horizontal.3`, remembered per Activity, default Report). **Report**: ring + "7 – 10am · Perfect" + the hour strip with integers + "Your hours 6 – 10am". **Explore**: ring + `−` hour pills `+` + rows **Your hours** (range · Average N) / **Best hours** (window · verdict) / **Looking at** (span · Average N, only when the span differs, highlighted) + a full-width "Back to 6 – 10am" pill when it differs. The looked-at span's score comes from the existing `DashboardViewModel.scoreSpan` server call. The shipped "Average of … · tap an hour" caption and the Avg/hour segment are **removed**; the metric cards keep averaging the shown span.
- **Coming days** list on the tomorrow detail (after the hours passed): the days after the shown one — dot · day · "7 – 10am · Perfect" / "No good time" (red) · "Average N" right-aligned.
- **"No good time"** everywhere a null day is named (card, rows, list).
- **Layout rules (owner, 2026-11-15/16)**: one padding — **16pt** inside every container, 16pt between containers, 16pt page margin; inner gaps 4 / 8 / 16. **Superellipse corners** on every rounded container (`RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius:, style: .continuous)`); nested radii **outer = inner + padding** → inner r8 (rows, highlight band, back pill), outer r24 (cards, hero, metric cards, hours-over row, list, overlay); switch thumb r13 in r15. No dead space: the ring column ends level with the text column. The ring numeral is centred in the ring; the caption is 13 semibold `text/secondary` below it. The one allowed exception: the Explore rows' highlight band (12 / 6 inset).
- Round "+" button, the Updated footer, the beta ⓘ, the opening, the wizard, Settings: **unchanged**.
